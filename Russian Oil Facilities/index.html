🔍 Key Strategic Insights

🏭 Refinery Concentration Risk

Russia's top 10 refineries process 65% of all crude. Even partial damage to Ryazan, Saratov, or TANECO causes nationwide fuel disruptions. Ryazan refinery alone supplies ~30% of Moscow's fuel.

⚠️ Military Fuel Dependency

The Russian military consumes an estimated 2–3 million tonnes of fuel per month in Ukraine. Sustained strikes on depots and refineries create a measurable logistics bottleneck at the front line.

🔧 Repair Lag

Ukrainian strikes have specifically targeted control systems and heat exchangers, which take 3–9 months to replace due to Western sanctions on spare parts. Russia cannot source these quickly.

💸 $185B Revenue Under Threat

At sustained campaign pace, Russia's oil export revenue could fall by $15–25B annually by 2027. Combined with rising war costs, this could force hard choices between military spending and civilian fuel subsidies.
