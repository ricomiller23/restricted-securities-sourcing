# Development Notes: 0.006 Delisted CRM Database

## Architecture Notes
- The Delisted CRM Database frontend is deployed to Vercel at `https://delisted-crm-database.vercel.app`.
- Tracks 1,704+ distressed, suspended, and delisted OTC & Nasdaq corporate issuers with legal counsel, auditor profiles, and contact details.
- Integrates with EDGAR Insider Scout and restricted sourcing pipelines for daily synchronization.
