> **Item 4 – Excluded Assets and Liabilities:**  
> *"Except as expressly assumed in the Asset Purchase Agreement, SNTX shall not assume any liabilities of GTVH. All liabilities, debts, obligations, contingent liabilities, litigation claims, **TAX LIABILITIES** and legacy obligations of GTVH arising before or after the Effective Date shall remain solely with GTVH."*

* **The Tax Evasion Mechanism**: Management intentionally transferred all revenue streams (Go Fast Beverages, Snapt Beverages, Deep South Electric, Golden Era, and IP) into SNTX while leaving all existing and future **tax liabilities** behind in GTVH. #### 2. 26 U.S.C. § 7201: Felony "Evasion of Payment"
Under Supreme Court and Federal Circuit precedents (*Spies v. United States*, 317 U.S. 492; *United States v. Conley*, 826 F.2d 551; *United States v. Hook*, 781 F.2d 1166), **Evasion of Payment** under § 7201 occurs when a taxpayer engages in **affirmative acts of concealment or asset transfers to corporate shells** to defeat the collection of known or anticipated tax liabilities.
* **Affirmative Act #1**: Transferring 100% of corporate operating assets on April 16, 2026, to a related sister company.
* **Affirmative Act #2**: Explicitly designating "tax liabilities" as excluded liabilities left in an insolvent shell.
* **Affirmative Act #3**: Operating both entities under common insider control (Javier Leal) while defaulting on all legitimate creditor and operational debts for over three (3) consecutive months ($300,000+).

#### 3. 26 U.S.C. § 7206(4): Concealment of Property Subject to IRS Levy
Under 26 U.S.C. § 6321 and § 6331, the IRS possesses statutory lien and levy rights against all property and rights to property belonging to a corporate taxpayer (GTVH). Moving the physical operations, customer contracts, and intellectual property of Go Fast Beverages, Snapt Beverages, and Deep South Electric into SNTX constitutes a direct felony violation of § 7206(4) by removing and concealing property upon which levy is authorized.

#### 4. IRC § 1060 & Form 8594: Unreported $4,000,000 Asset Sale Gain
* **Transaction Valuation**: Item 2 of the June 15 Disclosure states that as consideration for the assets, SNTX issued **400,000,000 common shares** valued at **$4,000,000.00** ($0.01 market price).
* **Statutory Requirement**: Under IRC § 1060 and Treasury Reg. § 1.1060-1, both the buyer (SNTX) and seller (GTVH) are legally required to file **IRS Form 8594 (Asset Acquisition Statement)** with their federal income tax returns, allocating the $4M purchase price among Class I–VII assets (goodwill, equipment, intangibles) and recognizing taxable capital gains and depreciation recapture.
* **Tax Fraud**: GTVH and SNTX structured this deal to distribute 400M shares to shareholders under Rule 144 without paying the requisite corporate-level capital gains tax on the $4,000,000 disposition, evading hundreds of thousands of dollars in federal corporate income tax.

#### 5. 26 U.S.C. § 61(a)(11) & § 108: Unreported Cancellation of Debt (COD) Income
On March 29, 2026, GTVH executed a Settlement Agreement with Bruce Perlowin compromising $4.4M in land use and joint venture claims down to $300,000. Under IRC § 61(a)(11), debt forgiveness generates taxable Cancellation of Debt (COD) income. GTVH then defaulted on the entire $300,000 for 3 consecutive months while stripping assets. Falsely claiming insolvency exclusions under IRC § 108 while siphoning $4M in assets to an insider-controlled sister company constitutes fraudulent tax reporting.

#### 6. Constructive Dividends & Executive Self-Dealing (IRC §§ 301, 316)
