2. Functional Deep Dive by Investment Strategy

A. Strategy 1: Cross-Border Restructuring & SEC Section 3(a)(10) Recognition

Under Section 3(a)(10) of the US Securities Act of 1933, shares issued in exchange for outstanding creditor claims or debt securities are exempt from registration if approved following a formal fairness hearing by an authorized judicial body. In the UK, Part 26 Schemes and Part 26A Restructuring Plans (*CIGA 2020*) sanctioned by the High Court of Justice in London hold decades of formal SEC No-Action precedent. In Australia, Part 5.1 s411 Schemes and Part 5.3A DOCAs fulfill the exact same fairness standard before Supreme and Federal Courts. In Germany, StaRUG and § 225a InsO Insolvency Plans provide binding cross-class cram down (*Debt-to-Equity*) mechanisms recognized across European and US cross-border capital structures.

B. Strategy 2: Delisted & Suspended Entities CRM (Reverse Takeovers & Removal Clocks)

Shell companies and suspended vehicles represent prime targets for non-dilutive backdoor listings (RTOs). On the London Stock Exchange (AIM), companies disposing of operations are classified as AIM Rule 15 Cash Shells and must complete an RTO within 6 months or face trading suspension and cancellation under AIM Rule 41. On the ASX, Listing Rule 17.12 establishes a strict 2-year automatic removal countdown clock from suspension. In Frankfurt, clean German AG vehicles (*Börsenmäntel*) offer expedited quotation access across the Freiverkehr (Basic Board and Scale) segments.

C. Strategy 3: Balance Sheet Distress Screener & Capital Outreach

Statutory distress triggers alert investors to urgent liquidity needs prior to formal insolvency:

  • UK Section 656 Companies Act 2006: When a public company's net assets fall to $\le 50\%$ of called-up share capital, directors are legally obligated to convene an EGM within 28 days to consider emergency recapitalization.
  • German § 92 AktG: Management (*Vorstand*) must immediately convene an Extraordinary General Meeting the moment half the share capital is lost.
  • Australian Appendix 5B/4C Item 8.6: Exploration and technology companies reporting < 2.0 quarters of cash runway must formally detail their capital raising plans to the ASX.

D. Strategy 4: Insider Signal Scouting, Major Holdings & Dilution Radars

Tracking high-conviction insider buying alongside major institutional accumulations:

  • Director Dealings: UK MAR Article 19 (PDMRs), Australian Appendix 3Y, and German EU MAR Article 19 require open-market trades by board members to be disclosed within 3 to 5 business days.
  • Substantial Shareholder Infiltration: UK TR-1 and German WpHG mandate disclosures starting at 3% (and every 1% increment thereafter in the UK), while Australia's Form 603 triggers at 5%.
  • Dilution Radar: Monitors annual statutory pre-emption rights disapplication authorities (UK Section 570 CA 2006, ASX LR 7.1/7.1A 15%+10% placement capacity, and German § 186 AktG 10% non-pre-emptive placement limits).
